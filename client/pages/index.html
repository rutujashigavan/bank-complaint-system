Help Center

🔐 Login & Account Issues
How do I login?
Enter your registered email and password on the login page and click Login.
I forgot my password. What should I do?
Click on Forgot Password and follow the steps to reset your password via email.
Why is my account locked?
Too many failed login attempts may lock your account temporarily for security.
📝 Complaint Registration
How do I register a complaint?
Login, open Raise Complaint, choose category, fill required fields and submit.
What details are required?
Name, email, account number, complaint type, and description.
Can I edit my complaint?
Currently editing is not allowed after submission.
📊 Complaint Status
How do I check complaint status?
Login and go to My Complaints to view the status.
What does Pending mean?
Your complaint is still under review.
What does Resolved mean?
Your complaint has been successfully solved.
💳 Banking Issues
Money deducted but not received?
Report it under Transaction Issue immediately.
UPI failed but money deducted?
Usually auto-refunded within 24–48 hours.
ATM not dispensing cash?
Amount will usually be refunded within 3–7 working days.
